Pop-a-Pop Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game Pop-a-Pop (the "game"), developed by Rıza Yüksel ("I", "me"). It explains what information the game collects, how it is used, and what choices you have.

1. Summary

The game has no accounts. It does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, camera, microphone or precise location. Some information is collected automatically by third-party services (Google Firebase and Google AdMob) to understand how the game is used, fix crashes and show ads.

2. Information collected

  • Game progress and settings (stars earned, volume settings) are stored only on your device.
  • Usage analytics (Firebase Analytics): in-game actions such as levels started and finished, results, pausing and settings changes, together with device and app information (device model, operating system version, language, app version, approximate location at country or city level derived from your IP address) and a random app instance identifier.
  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics): technical information when the game crashes, such as device model, operating system version, app state and an installation identifier.
  • Advertising (Google AdMob): your device's advertising ID, IP address, device information and ad interaction data, used to deliver ads and measure them.
  • Notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging): a device token used to send you announcements, for example when new levels are released. Notifications require your permission on Android 13 and later.

3. How the information is used

  • To understand how the game is played and improve levels and difficulty.
  • To find and fix crashes and technical problems.
  • To show and measure advertising, which keeps the game free.
  • To send notifications about game updates, if you allow them.

4. Consent

When you first open the game you are asked whether you agree to the collection of analytics data and to advertising data use. If you decline, analytics and personalized advertising are turned off, and any ads shown will be non-personalized. You can withdraw your consent at any time by clearing the game's data in your device settings or by uninstalling the game. You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your Android settings.

5. Sharing

Information is processed by Google LLC and its affiliates as service providers (Firebase and AdMob), and by advertising partners involved in showing ads through AdMob. I do not sell your personal information. For details see:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Privacy and Security in Firebase
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

6. Data retention and deletion

Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months. Crash reports are kept for a limited period, typically up to 90 days. Game progress stored on your device is deleted when you uninstall the game. Because the collected data is not linked to your name or email address, I may not be able to identify data that belongs to you. You can request deletion of data that can be associated with you by contacting me (see Contact). I will respond within a reasonable time, and no later than 30 days.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Turkey), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its processing, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your local data protection authority. To exercise these rights, contact me.

8. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, or under the minimum age required to consent to data processing in your country. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact me and I will take steps to delete it.

9. Security

Data is transmitted to Google services over encrypted connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but I rely on the security measures of the services described above.

10. Changes to this policy

I may update this policy when the game or the services it uses change. The date at the top shows the latest update.
